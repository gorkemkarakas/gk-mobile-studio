Overview

GK Mobile Studio provides TripLedger as an expense tracking, travel budgeting, subscription management and shared expense tracking application.

App Data and Accounts

TripLedger stores trip, expense, person/member, debt and subscription data locally in the app (localStorage/WebView). These records are not sent to a TripLedger backend server; the app has no backend server or user accounts. Android backup mechanisms are described below.

Exchange-Rate Service

The app requests currency rates from the third-party Frankfurter service over HTTPS. This is a fixed currency query: trip names, expense descriptions, person names, debt and subscription records are not included. TripLedger financial records are not transmitted in these requests. The provider may receive standard connection information such as an IP address. External websites opened from the app have their own privacy policies.

Local Notifications

Subscription and payment reminders are scheduled as local notifications on the device. Cloud push services and Firebase are not used for these reminders; reminder content is not sent to a push server.

Android Backup

The Android app permits operating-system backup (android:allowBackup=true). Depending on your operating-system, device and backup settings, Android may back up app data or transfer it to another device. App data is therefore not guaranteed to remain on the same physical device at all times.

Analytics and Advertising

No user tracking/analytics SDK or advertising SDK is active. In-app expense charts are generated from local data. The app does not request location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions.
