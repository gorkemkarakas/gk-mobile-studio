Overview

GK Mobile Studio provides TinyNotes as a minimal note taking application focused on simple writing and organization.

Information You Provide

TinyNotes may store notes and related content you create in the app. This information is used to provide note taking features.

Device and App Data

The app may use technical information required for core functionality, diagnostics, security, crash handling and performance improvement, depending on the services enabled in the app version you use.

How Information Is Used

  • To create, display and manage notes.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the application.
  • To respond to support requests sent by email.

Third-Party Services

TinyNotes may rely on platform or analytics services where necessary for app distribution, diagnostics or performance. Third-party services process data according to their own privacy practices.
